Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-04

tiannanhaibei.com (the "Site") is a free playground. Everyone is welcome to come and have fun. We respect your privacy. This policy explains what data we collect, why, how we use and protect it, and what rights you have.

1. Data we collect

  • Registration data: email address, nickname, password.
  • Content you provide: blog posts, forum posts, comments, and the content of chat messages and private messages.
  • Accommodation listings: if you post in the "Stays" section, it may include a location and contact details.
  • Automatically collected data: IP address, browser type, visit time and pages viewed, used for security and statistics.
  • Cookies: used to keep you signed in.

The Site does not ask for national ID numbers, credit card numbers or bank account numbers. Please do not post them on the Site.

Public and private content

  • Public: blogs, forum posts, comments and Stays listings can be seen by anyone. Do not post anything there that you do not want others to know.
  • Private messages and chat: only the sender and the recipient can see them on the Site. However, they are stored in plain text in the database, and the Site administrator technically has access. We do not look at them proactively, except to handle reports, maintain security or comply with legal requirements.

2. How we use data

  • To provide accounts and sign-in
  • To display content you post
  • To keep the Site secure and prevent abuse and spam
  • To improve the Site
  • To contact you when needed, for example password reset or important notices

3. Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We share it only:

  • When required by law, or when a government authority makes a lawful request.
  • To protect the security of the Site and its users.
  • With service providers such as hosting and email, who are required to keep it confidential.

4. Storage and security

The Site is hosted on Cloudflare Pages (Cloudflare, Inc., headquartered in the United States, with data centers worldwide). User registration data and content are stored with a third-party cloud service provider (servers in the United States or other regions). We take reasonable measures such as HTTPS encryption. No system on the internet is completely secure; if a data breach occurs, we will notify affected users as soon as possible.

We keep your data while your account exists. After you delete your account, we will delete your data within 30 days, except where the law requires us to keep it.

5. Your rights

Depending on the law where you live, you may ask to: access your data, get a copy, correct or add to it, stop its use, or delete it. Email richujianghua888@gmail.com and we will reply within a reasonable time.

6. Minors

The Site is not open to anyone under 18. If we find an account belonging to a minor, we will delete it.

7. External links

The Site may link to other websites. We do not control their privacy practices.

8. Changes to this policy

Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date. Major changes will be announced on the Site.
